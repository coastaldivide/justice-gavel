# Justice Gavel — Top Marketing Ideas (pitches & social ads)

Owner-approved messaging, October 2026. Use these exact lines; they are the
product, not a promise beyond it.

## The three core lines

| Line | Role | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Justice, in your hands.** | Brand tagline (keep everywhere) | App icon caption, store headline, website hero, end card of every ad |
| **The single app for the worst few days of your life.** | Marketing line — talking *to* the person | App store subtitle, social ads, landing page |
| **The single app for the worst few days of your loved one's life.** | Marketing line — families | Ads aimed at parents, partners, siblings |
| **The single app for the worst few days of someone's life.** | Marketing line — talking *about* users | Investor decks, press, About page |

## The core path (product = message)

> **Just arrested?**
> 1. Know your rights
> 2. Find a lawyer **and** a bail agent
> 3. Never miss a court date

"And" is deliberate: an arrested person usually needs both, and no single-purpose
competitor gives them together.

## Why it wins (the one-sentence positioning)

Every competitor owns one slice — AI answers, lawyer directories, bail marketplaces,
court-reminder texting, record clearing. Justice Gavel is the only one built for the
whole moment, for the person arrested and the family helping them.

## Pitch deck — the slides these lines anchor

1. **Title:** Justice Gavel — Justice, in your hands.
2. **Problem:** The worst few days of someone's life, spent on six websites at 2 a.m.
3. **Solution:** One app — the three-step core path, one screenshot per step.
4. **Why now / why us:** Competitors each own one slice (Rasa, Uptrust, Vikk, Lawhive,
   free AI chat, directories); nobody owns the moment.
5. **Traction:** (fill with real numbers only — downloads, weekly active users,
   attorneys/agents signed up, court dates tracked).
6. **Business model:** Free for people in crisis; attorneys and bail agents pay
   (subscriptions + flat lead fees: bail agents $100, PI/civil rights $200).
7. **Ask:** Small pre-seed party round (see investor notes).

## Rules for every ad and pitch

- Show only features that work on a real phone today (see the Real-World Test Script).
- No legal-outcome promises ("get out of jail", "beat your charge") — the app gives
  information and connections, not legal advice.
- No fake numbers, ratings or testimonials. Use real metrics or none.
- Lawyer/bail-agent ads must follow state bar advertising rules; have a lawyer review
  the first campaign.
